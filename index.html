How It Works

Three simple steps to report, identify, and recover missing items on campus.

1

Report Item

Submit specific details about the item you lost or discovered on campus, including location, approximate time, identifying characteristics, and photographs.

2

Match & Verify

Our automated matching engine correlates reports in real time. Submit an ownership claim backed by identifying proof, distinctive marks, or purchase receipts.

3

Safe Recovery

Complete an administrator-verified handover at designated campus points. Every verified return is recorded in tamper-evident audit logs.
